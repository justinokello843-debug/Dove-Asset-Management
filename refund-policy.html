Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Dove Asset Management provides professional real estate and investment management services, including portfolio and facilities management, leasing advisory, capital markets advisory, software subscriptions, and investment management delivered through our subsidiary, Halcyon Investment Management. As these are professional services rather than physical goods, refunds and cancellations are handled as set out below.

Advisory and management fees

Fees are billed against specific engagements or invoices issued to the client. Where a service has not yet commenced, a client may request cancellation of the corresponding invoice by contacting us within 7 days of payment, and a full refund will be issued to the original payment method. Once a service has commenced or been delivered — including advisory work performed, portfolio management activities undertaken, or leasing introductions made — fees are non-refundable, reflecting the professional time and resources already committed on the client's behalf.

Recurring and subscription fees

This applies to software subscriptions and ongoing management retainers. Clients may cancel future billing at any time by written notice to us. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period; fees already paid for the current period are non-refundable, but no further charges will be applied after cancellation.

Payment errors or duplicate charges

Any duplicate or erroneous charge will be refunded in full upon verification. Clients should report such issues within 30 days of the transaction, along with the relevant invoice or reference number, so we can investigate and resolve it promptly.

Processing time

Approved refunds are processed within 5–10 business days of approval, though the funds may take additional time to reflect in your account depending on your bank or payment provider's own processing schedule.

Contact us

For any refund or cancellation request, please reach us at support@doveassetmanagement or +254 753 221960.
